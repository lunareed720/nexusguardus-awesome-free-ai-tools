## Dev.to Article

**Title:** The $0 AI Stack: How I replaced $300/month of subscriptions with free tools

**Body:**

I recently cancelled $300/month of AI subscriptions — GitHub Copilot, Midjourney, RunwayML, and 4 others — and replaced them with free/open-source alternatives.

The result: nearly identical output quality, zero recurring cost.

### 🤖 AI Chat

I switched from Claude Pro to a combo of Ollama (local Llama 4) + Poe free tier (1,000 msg/day).

### 🎨 AI Image

Cancelled Midjourney ($30/mo) and now use:
- InvokeAI (self-hosted) — unlimited image generation
- Fooocus — simplest UX

### 🎬 AI Video

Cancelled RunwayML ($28/mo), now using:
- Deforum (open-source, runs locally)
- AnimateDiff for image-to-video

### 🔧 AI Code

Cancelled GitHub Copilot ($10/mo), now using:
- Continue (OSS, self-hosted)
- AIDER (terminal-based, open source)

### 🧹 What I Gained Besides Saving $300/mo

- No rate limits
- No usage tracking
- Full control over my data
- No provider lock-in
